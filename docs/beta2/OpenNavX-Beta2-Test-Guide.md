# Checking your SKAGER installation

After installing an official package:

1. Confirm the version in Diagnostics.
2. Verify that chart content loads and that pan and zoom work.
3. Check that your existing routes and waypoints remain available.
4. Check available instruments against your existing onboard displays. Confirm
   that disconnected sensors show unavailable or stale data.
5. Check Day, Dusk and Night presentation and readability at your display size.
6. Open Legacy and Safe modes, then return to SKAGER and verify charts again.
7. Check Diagnostics and retain any exact error message if a problem occurs.

Keep checks read-only until hardware commissioning has been completed for your
equipment. Do not rely on advisory estimates as a navigation guarantee.

Report problems through https://beta.skager.app/support/. Include the product
version, Windows version, reproduction steps and relevant screenshots. Review
logs and diagnostic bundles for personal information before sharing them.
